Competition distance

Average store sales by distance band (95% CI omitted in mockup)
Stores <500 m from a competitor average more, but the overall rank correlation is approx. 0 (rho -0.03, p 0.35): location mix, not a competition effect.
Insight for the category team: promotions coincide with roughly +39% sales and +21% traffic, but the calendar is not random — use this page to size the opportunity, not to claim a causal lift. The weekly pattern (Monday peak, Saturday trough) is the most reliable operational lever.
